## Methodology & caveats

- Every count below is **IRES-owned units only**. Where IRES owns part of a larger scheme,
  only its share is catalogued (e.g. The Marker = 85 two-bed apartments owned, not the
  105-unit building; Beacon South Quarter = 213 owned, not 880).
- Bedroom breakdowns are taken from each development's own page, which reports the IRES-owned
  mix directly.
- Extracted via automated page-fetch through a summarisation model — best-effort, not a
  pixel-perfect scrape. Verify raw pages if exact figures matter.
- **Source anomalies (reported, not corrected):**
  - **Belville Court** — published mix sums to 19 (1:3, 2:14, 3:2) vs. stated 15 owned;
    this +4 is why the aggregate mix below totals 3,615 vs. the 3,611 headline.
  - **Heywood Court** — only 1-bed (4) and 2-bed (35) broken out (= 39 owned); page text
    mentions 3-beds exist in the scheme but gives no owned 3-bed count.
- Only two studios recorded: The School Yard (5) and Tara View (2).
